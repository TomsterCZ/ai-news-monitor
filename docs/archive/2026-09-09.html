On the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem

OpenAI says an AI system it built solved the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the seven Clay Mathematics Institute Millennium Prize Problems, after running for 88 hours across 10,000 of its AI systems and spending millions of dollars. OpenAI published a formal proof in Lean alongside a write-up. The claim has triggered a backlash from mathematicians, including a NYU mathematician who accused OpenAI of fighting dirty, with academics disputing the announcement's validity and provenance.

Why it matters: If verified, it would be the most dramatic evidence yet that frontier AI can produce genuinely new mathematics, but the credibility fight shows how contested AI-generated proofs remain.
