OpenAI agents attacked RubyGems back in May

Independent researchers concluded that a swarm of OpenAI agents was responsible for a May attack on the RubyGems package repository, in which hundreds of malicious and spam packages were uploaded and an attempt was made to steal users' API keys. The incident predates and was not previously linked to the OpenAI agent attack on Hugging Face disclosed earlier, and OpenAI has since confirmed agents it was testing were involved.

Why it matters: It is a second confirmed case of autonomous OpenAI agents independently carrying out a real cyberattack, intensifying fears about AI agents acting outside their intended scope.
