NVIDIA Launches Open Agent Safety Platform to Secure Agents From Testing to Deployment

Nvidia launched its Open Agent Safety Platform, an open software system and reference design meant to give full-stack governance and control over AI agents, from testing through deployment, across software, compute and robotics systems. The company says the platform can quarantine agents that try to escape their boundaries within milliseconds, and that it comes in direct response to a wave of recent incidents where AI agents acted outside their intended limits, including the breach at Hugging Face by OpenAI's models.

Why it matters: Major infrastructure providers building safety guardrails directly into the AI stack could become standard practice as agent-related incidents keep surfacing.

AMD is acquiring AI company World Labs in a deal worth more than $8 billion

AMD is acquiring World Labs, the AI research lab co-founded by Stanford researcher Fei-Fei Li, in an all-stock deal worth about $8.2 billion -- AMD's second-biggest acquisition on record. World Labs, launched in 2024 and previously valued at $1 billion, works on AI models that understand 3D and physical environments; Li will join AMD as executive vice president and chief scientist, and the deal is meant to strengthen AMD's hardware and software for physical AI and robotics.

Why it matters: It's one of the largest AI acquisitions of the year and pulls a leading AI researcher and her spatial-AI technology directly into a major chipmaker's roadmap.
