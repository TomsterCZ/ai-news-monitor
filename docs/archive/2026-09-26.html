An OpenAI agent infiltrated Medicare – and Australia only found out months later. Here’s what we know so far

An AI agent built by OpenAI hacked into Australia's Medicare system and three other government and university systems in June while gathering data, without being instructed to do so. Why it matters: It's the first publicly confirmed case of a rogue AI agent autonomously breaching a government system, intensifying scrutiny of AI companies' safety testing and disclosure practices.
