AI firm Anthropic to commit 5% of shares to fund for ‘public good’

Anthropic will commit 5% of its shares, worth roughly $48bn at its May valuation, to a new fund for the "public good," subject to shareholder approval. Why it matters: It's one of the largest pledges yet by a frontier AI lab to share the financial upside of AI with the public ahead of a major IPO.
