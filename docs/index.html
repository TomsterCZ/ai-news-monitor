Anthropic S-1 Reveals Rapid Growth Ahead of Blockbuster IPO

Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 prospectus ahead of what could be the largest IPO ever, disclosing revenue that soared to $16.3 billion in the first half of the year and plans to spend $807 billion on AI infrastructure. The filing, which lists on Nasdaq under ticker ANT, also details an unusual governance structure giving the seven founders majority voting control and devotes roughly 100 pages to risk factors, including what it calls catastrophic or existential risks to humanity.

Why it matters: It is the first detailed look at the finances of a frontier AI lab and a major test of whether the AI boom's spending can be justified to public markets.
